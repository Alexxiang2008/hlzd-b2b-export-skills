## 2. GEOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

The Kwanza Basin is a major sedimentary basin located along the western coastline of Angola, with its capital city being Luanda. It is one of the most significant hydrocarbon-producing regions in the country. The Kwanza Basin is an elongated rift basin that formed during the Early Cretaceous period because of extensional tectonic forces. It is situated within the larger West African Rift System, which extends along the western coast of Africa. The basin is characterized by a thick accumulation of sedimentary rocks, including sandstones, shales, and carbonates. The Kwanza Basin presents geological challenges due to its complex structural history and the presence of salt diapirs. Salt structures can cause complications in drilling operations and reservoir imaging.
