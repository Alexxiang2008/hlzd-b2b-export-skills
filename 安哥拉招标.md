A1. Complete Invitation to Bid (ITB) / Tender Document
    

A2. Well Engineering Basis of Design
    • Number of wells to be drilled (and whether exploratory,
      appraisal, or development)
    • Well type (vertical / directional / horizontal / multilateral)
    • Well schematic / casing programme (conductor, surface,
      intermediate, production sizes and depths)
    • Target formations and TD confirmation
    

A3. Geological Risk Package
    • Offset well history (drilling time, events, problems)
    • Pore pressure, fracture pressure, collapse pressure gradients
    • H2S / CO2 content (per ISO 15156 / NACE MR0175)
    • Geothermal gradient and BHCT estimate
    

A4. Detailed Rig Technical Specification
    • Drawworks horsepower and hookload rating
    • Mast / substructure rated capacity
    • Mud pump configuration (number × power × liner sizes)
    • Top drive (mandatory or optional; model)
    • BOP stack pressure rating (5000 / 10000 / 15000 psi)
    • Solids control package (shakers, desander, desilter,
      centrifuges)
    • Power generation and fuel storage
    

A5. Well Site and Logistics Information
    • Well coordinates (WGS84 preferred) for each well location
    • Road access (load-bearing capacity, distance from nearest port)
    • Port of entry (likely Luanda) and onward logistics
    • Camp facilities (current capacity, power, water, catering)
    • Communications coverage
   

A6. Draft Contract / Key Commercial Terms
    • Currency of bid (USD / EUR / Kz) and exchange risk allocation
    • Payment schedule and credit terms (mobilisation advance %,
      monthly invoicing, net 30 / 60 / 90)
    • Performance bond (amount, format, issuing banks accepted)
    • Liquidated damages / NPT penalties (caps and exclusions)
    • Force majeure scope (political, pandemic, strike)
    • Insurance requirements (well control, third-party liability,
      war risk, employer's liability)
    • Governing law and dispute resolution (London arbitration /
      ICC / Luanda courts)
  

A7. Cost Incidence Document (CID) / Service Split
    For each line item confirm whether it is supplied by the Contractor
    or by ANPG / the Operator:
    • Casing and wellhead
    • Cementing services
    • Mud materials and chemicals
    • Directional drilling tools and MWD/LWD
    • Wireline logging
    • Fishing services
    • Drill bits
    

A8. HSE Entry Requirements
    • Mandatory certifications (API Spec Q1/Q2, ISO 45001, ISO 14001)
    • Acceptable safety performance indicators (TRIR threshold)
    • ANPG-specific HSE operating rules
    • Emergency response expectations (medical evacuation, well
      control, H2S response)
    

A9. Local Content & Compliance Requirements
    • Local employment ratio (Angolan nationals %)
    • Equipment import duties and temporary admission (ATA Carnet)
    • Work visa quotas and processing timelines
    • Local banking and foreign exchange controls
